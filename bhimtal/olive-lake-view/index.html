Why Stay Here

A Stay Crafted Around You — Olive Lake View

At Olive Lake View, every detail is designed around warm Indian hospitality — welcome refreshments, evening hi-tea, dining and experiences curated for Bhimtal.

Bhimtal's lakeside boutique on the banks of the largest lake of Kumaon — 30 rooms (Deluxe, Triple Bed, Duplex Triple) on 8 June State Road, Mountain Club Road. Spacious, airy, lake-view balconies with sitting space. Fully modern conveniences, Wi-Fi, restaurant, 1 PM check-in. Ideal for families/couples seeking comfortable lake view stay near Nainital (25 min to Sattal Lake). 8 km from Nainital center, tranquil hills and pine forests.
