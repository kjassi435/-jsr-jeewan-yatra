Why Stay Here

A Stay Crafted Around You — Corbett Prakarm Resort

At Corbett Prakarm Resort, every detail is designed around warm Indian hospitality — welcome refreshments, evening hi-tea, dining and experiences curated for Sawaldey.

12-room jungle lodge in Sawaldey — 4 Deluxe, 4 Suite, 4 Luxury Cottages wrapped in Corbett's quieter Dhela-Jhirna corner. Simple sincere Kumaoni plates, tandoor and bonfire nights. 3-star value stay for wildlife lovers seeking wild hush over luxury frills, 15 km from Ramnagar with easy safari gate access.
