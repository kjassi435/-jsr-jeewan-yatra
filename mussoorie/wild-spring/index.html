Why Stay Here

A Stay Crafted Around You — Wild Spring

At Wild Spring, every detail is designed around warm Indian hospitality — welcome refreshments, evening hi-tea, dining and experiences curated for Mussoorie.

Mussoorie's boutique hideaway at Library Chowk, Spring Road — 14 valley-view rooms (Elegent & Attit) on the hill brow above Doon valley. Library, terrace, restaurant, free parking, valley-view terraces, yoga & hiking trails, infinity pool (seasonal). 29.8 km from Dehradun station, 56.2 km from Jolly Grant Airport. Farm-fresh Kumaoni & wood-fired plates. Rated 4.2/5 (247 reviews), loved for cozy fireplaces and farm-fresh food. Hill calm with Mall Road minutes away.
